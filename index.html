About

I'm a Team Leader of Digital Products Development at NASFUND, Papua New Guinea's National Superannuation Fund. I lead Laravel backend, mobile and React frontend developers building products for members and employers.

Before NASFUND I was a Senior Applications Developer at Vodafone PNG. I studied Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of Papua New Guinea.

Through Zuqongtech, my Port Moresby software consultancy, I build Laravel/PHP systems for clients, with WordPress, React/Next.js and AWS as supporting skills.

I'm active in the PNG tech community and publish reusable tooling as open source.
